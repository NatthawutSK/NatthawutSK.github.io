Gym Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Who uses this app

Gym Tracker is a personal Android app built and used by a single person. It is not published on any app store.

What data the app accesses

  • Your Google account's basic profile, to sign you in.
  • The one Google Sheet you choose in the app, through the Google Sheets API (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets). The app reads and writes only the workout tabs of that Sheet: exercises, weekly schedule and workout logs.

Where the data is stored

Workout data is stored on your device and in your own Google Sheet. The app has no server of its own and does not send data anywhere else.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. It contains no advertising and no analytics.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page. Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on the device; the data in your Google Sheet stays under your control.
