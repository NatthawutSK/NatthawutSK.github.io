Dog-Jod Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Who uses this app

Dog-Jod is a personal Android app used by its developer and a few people known to them. It is not published on any app store.

What data the app accesses

  • Your Google account's basic profile, to sign you in.
  • The Google Sheet the app creates for you, through the Google Sheets and Drive APIs (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). This scope lets the app see only files it created; it cannot see any other file in your Google Drive.
  • Images of bank transfer slips on your phone: ones you share to the app or pick in it, and, only if you switch on automatic detection, new images saved by your banking apps.
  • A Gemini API key that you create and enter yourself.

How the data is used

Each slip image is sent to the Google Gemini API, using your own API key, to read the date, amount, receiver and reference number. The result is written as a row in your Google Sheet. Gemini API use is governed by Google's terms for your key; on the free tier, Google may use the content to improve its products.

Where the data is stored

Expense rows are stored in your own Google Sheet and cached on your device. Slip images stay on your phone; the app does not upload them anywhere except to the Gemini API to be read. Your Gemini API key is stored encrypted on your device. The app has no server of its own.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. It contains no advertising and no analytics.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page. Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on the device; the data in your Google Sheet stays under your control.
